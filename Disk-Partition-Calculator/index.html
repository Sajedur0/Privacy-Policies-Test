Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Summary

  • •Your calculations, history and preferences stay only on your device.
  • •The app has no account, no sign-in, and no developer-operated server.
  • •The app requests no runtime permissions. The only network permission is INTERNET, used solely by Google Play's flexible in-app update check; your files, location and hardware are never accessed.
  • •The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.
  • •The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs — the only Google library is Play In-App Updates.
  • •The developer does not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.

Developer and contact

This policy covers the Disk Partition Calculator Android app (package: com.diskcalc). The developer is the publisher named on the app's Google Play Store listing.

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns, please contact the developer at the support email address shown on the app's Google Play Store listing.

Information the app handles

The following information is processed and stored only on your device:

  • •Calculation inputs: visible partition size in GB, total disk size in MB, partition count, filesystem type, Windows Setup simulation status, and custom Windows Setup overhead value.
  • •Calculation results and history: up to 100 most recent conversions, including the mode (Calc or Split), entered values, filesystem type, result values, text summary, timestamp, and the partition count for Split results.
  • •Each history entry also stores a randomly generated identifier (UUID). It is created on your device, is not a device or advertising identifier, and has no meaning outside the app.
  • •App preferences: theme selection (System, Light or Dark), default filesystem, default Windows Setup simulation status, selected Windows Setup profile, and custom overhead value.

Personal information

The app does not collect or ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or advertising ID. It does not require an account or any form of registration.

How the data is used

  • •To run partition-size calculations on your device.
  • •To show your saved history in the History sheet and let you copy or delete entries.
  • •To remember your preferences between app sessions.
  • •The data is not used for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

Data sharing and third parties

The developer does not share your data with third parties. The following features involve other services only when you explicitly use them:

  • •Google Play Services: the app uses Play In-App Updates (flexible flow with Play's native UI) to check for a newer version and, if you accept, download and install it automatically. That check sends no calculation, history or preference data. The 'Rate Us' button opens the Play Store listing; Google processes those interactions under its own privacy policy. Apart from AndroidX/Jetpack and Play In-App Updates, the app bundles no other third-party SDKs — no advertising, analytics, attribution, crash-reporting or social-media SDK.
  • •Android share sheet: The 'Share App' feature sends only a static Play Store link to the app you choose. That app handles the link under its own privacy policy. Your calculations and history are not attached or shared.
  • •Android backup: the app opts out of Android backup (allowBackup=false), so history and preferences are not backed up or transferred to another device.
  • •Clipboard: The Copy buttons place calculation results on your clipboard only when you tap them. The app does not read from the clipboard or access any other clipboard content.

Security

  • •History and preferences are kept in app-private storage, which Android's app sandbox keeps away from other apps.
  • •The app does not send your calculations, history or preferences over the network.
  • •The app contains no cloud-sync code; the only network code is the Google Play in-app update check.
  • •No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Retention and deletion

  • •History: up to 100 entries are kept. When you save more, the oldest entry is removed automatically. History stays until you delete it.
  • •Preferences: kept until you change them or clear the app's data.
  • •Delete one history entry: open History and tap the delete icon on that entry.
  • •Delete all history: open Settings, then History, then Clear, and confirm.
  • •Delete all app data: open Android Settings, then Apps, then Disk Partition Calculator, then Storage, then Clear storage. Uninstalling the app also removes its private data from the device.
  • •Android backup: the app opts out of backup, so no copy is created in device or Google backups.
  • •The developer does not hold copies of your data, so there is nothing on the developer's side to request or delete. The app has no account, so there is no account to delete.

Children

This is a general-purpose utility for Windows users and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • •You can view, delete or change your history and preferences at any time inside the app.
  • •Android backup is disabled for this app, so nothing leaves the device.
  • •You can stop using the app and uninstall it at any time.
  • •For questions about rights that apply to you under local privacy laws, contact the developer using the details in the Developer and contact section.
  • •Because the app does not collect personal information, the developer holds no personal data about you to access, correct, export or delete, and there is nothing to opt out of.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The version in the app is the current one, and the date at the top shows when it was last updated.

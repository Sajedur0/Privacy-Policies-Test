This Privacy Policy describes how PinSaver - Video Downloader (“the App”, “we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Please read this document carefully.

1

Overview

PinSaver - Video Downloader is a utility application that lets users download publicly available video content from Pinterest links for personal, non-commercial use. The App is designed to collect as little data as possible and does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details to use its core features.

The App is intended only for downloading content that you own, have created, or have explicit permission to use. Please respect copyright laws and Pinterest’s Terms of Service. You are solely responsible for the content you download and how you use it.

2

Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear and transparent about what we do not collect. The App:

  • Does NOT require user accounts or registration.
  • Does NOT collect your name, email address, phone number, or any contact details.
  • Does NOT collect your location (GPS or network-based).
  • Does NOT track your browsing activity or online behavior.
  • Does NOT sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party.
  • Does NOT use advertising SDKs or serve targeted advertisements.
  • Does NOT use analytics SDKs, cookies, or fingerprinting techniques.
  • Does NOT collect any personal, financial, health, or sensitive information.
3

Information We Collect

3.1. Links You Paste (Processed Locally)

When you paste a Pinterest URL into the App to find a video, the URL is transmitted to Pinterest’s servers only to retrieve the media information (the public video file) you requested. The App does not store the URL or the content of the video on our servers. Any such request is made directly from your device, and Pinterest’s own Privacy Policy applies to those requests.

3.2. Downloaded Videos (Stored on Your Device)

Videos you download are saved to your device’s local storage (in the Download/PinSaver folder). These files remain on your device and are not uploaded to any server.

3.3. Technical Data (Automatically Processed by Your Device)

Standard, non-identifiable technical information (such as the operating system version, device model, and app version) may be processed automatically by the operating system and Google Play services in connection with app installation and updates. We do not receive or store this data.

4

Permissions We Request

The App requests the following permissions, and we explain exactly why each one is needed:

PermissionPurpose
InternetTo access the Pinterest page and download the video file you requested.
StorageTo save downloaded videos to your device and to manage (list, rename, share, delete) the files you have saved. On Android 10 and above, the App uses scoped storage and only accesses the files it creates in the Download/PinSaver folder. On Android 13 and above, the App requests the granular media permission (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO) only when you need to manage videos saved outside the App’s own folder.
NotificationsTo show you download progress and completion notifications while the App is downloading or converting a video. On Android 13 and above, the App asks for the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission, and you can deny or revoke it at any time.

All permissions are used only for the features described above. No permission is used to access any data other than the files you explicitly choose to download.

5

How We Use Your Information

The App uses the information described above solely for the following purposes:

  • To process the video download requests you make.
  • To save and organize downloaded files on your device.
  • To show download progress and status notifications.
  • To improve the App’s functionality and fix issues.

We do not use your information for marketing, profiling, or any other purpose.

6

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only external network activity is the direct request to Pinterest’s servers to retrieve the public media file you requested, subject to Pinterest’s own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy:

  • Pinterest Privacy Policy: policy.pinterest.com/en/privacy-policy
  • Pinterest Terms of Service: policy.pinterest.com/en/terms-of-service

We encourage you to review Pinterest’s policies, as their practices are not governed by this Privacy Policy.

7

Data Storage and Security

  • All downloaded content is stored locally on your own device.
  • The App does not maintain any database of your activity, and it does not transmit your data to any server operated by us.
  • When you delete a downloaded file through the App or the Android file system, it is removed from your device.
  • Because we do not collect or store your personal data on any server, there is no central database that could be compromised.
8

Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your Data: Since the App does not collect personal data, there is nothing for you to request deletion of from our servers.
  • Downloaded Files: These are stored on your device. You can delete them at any time using the “Delete Videos” option in the App’s Download History, or directly through the Android file manager.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the App removes the App’s data from your device. Please note that videos you saved to public storage will remain in your Download/PinSaver folder unless you delete them yourself.
